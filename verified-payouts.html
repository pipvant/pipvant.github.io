How a payout earns its place here

A payout becomes a record here only when a public source states the firm, the exact amount and the exact date — and the settlement is verifiable on a public blockchain. These records are tracked by Payout Junction, which matches firm payout wallets to on-chain transactions under a published methodology, and are quoted here with attribution under its stated terms. No date, no record: window totals, marketing headlines and undated claims are excluded by design.

FAQs

What makes a payout “verified” on this page?

It settled on a public blockchain and is recorded by Payout Junction, which matches firm payout wallets to on-chain transactions under a published methodology. A record is listed only with the firm, the exact amount, the exact date and a public source page — anything missing one of those is excluded.

Why is FTMO not on this page?

FTMO pays through bank and other off-chain rails that public trackers cannot observe. The same applies to Topstep, Apex Trader Funding and seven other firms in our database. Their absence means “not observable this way”, never “does not pay”.

Where does this data come from?

Payout Junction (payoutjunction.com), quoted with attribution under its stated terms. Every row links to the exact source page it was read from, and the capture date is printed at the top of this page.

Are these the biggest prop firm payouts ever?

They are the largest single blockchain-verified payouts in the tracked records set as of the capture date. Payouts on off-chain rails — including some very large ones — cannot be verified this way, and PIPVANT does not estimate or guess them.
