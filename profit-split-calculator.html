Profit split FAQs

How does a profit split work?

You keep your split percentage of every payout; the firm keeps the rest. On a $5,000 profit with an 80% split, you receive $4,000 and the firm keeps $1,000.

What is a good profit split?

80% is the industry standard; 90%+ is generous and usually tied to scaling plans or first payouts. Below 70% is only worth it if everything else — price, drawdown, payout speed — is excellent.

Are payouts taxed?

PIPVANT can't give tax advice — payout income is taxable in most countries. Ask a local accountant before your first big payout.
