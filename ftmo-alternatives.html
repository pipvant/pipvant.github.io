Why traders look beyond FTMO

Usually it comes down to four things: entry price (a $22 evaluation is a different commitment than $99+), payout speed (daily vs 14-day waits change cash flow), drawdown rules (static vs trailing decides how the challenge feels), and market focus (futures specialists vs CFD/forex firms). None of these make a firm "better" — they make it a better or worse fit for a specific trader. That's exactly what the free matcher filters on.

Price says nothing about legitimacy. Check payout proof, operating history, and whether rules are published transparently. PIPVANT only lists firms with verifiable data and review dates.
