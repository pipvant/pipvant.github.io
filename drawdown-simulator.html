Drawdown FAQs

What is the difference between static and trailing drawdown?

Static drawdown never moves: the floor is fixed at your starting balance minus the max drawdown. Trailing drawdown follows your highest balance upward, so the floor rises as you profit — you must protect open gains.

What is EOD trailing with a breakeven lock?

EOD (end-of-day) trailing only updates the floor at each day's close. On many firms it locks at your starting balance (breakeven) — it can trail upward to, but never above, where you started.

How is the daily loss limit measured?

Conventions differ by firm. This simulator measures it against the initial account size — the most common convention — so you can compare the worst case across firms.
