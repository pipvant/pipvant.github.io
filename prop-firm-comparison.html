How to choose a prop firm

Most traders pick a firm by price and regret it by rule. The three numbers that actually decide whether you pass: drawdown type (static is kinder than trailing), profit target vs drawdown ratio (a 10% target with 5% drawdown is twice as hard as it looks), and payout terms (minimum days, split, and method). PIPVANT's matcher lets you filter every tracked plan on exactly these dimensions — free, with verified data updated regularly.

What to compareWhy it matters
Drawdown typeTrailing drawdown follows your equity peaks — much harder than static
Profit targetLower targets (6-8%) are statistically far easier than 10%+
Evaluation feeSunk cost — never risk money you can't afford to lose on retries
Profit split80-90% to you is standard; scaling plans reward consistency
Payout speedFirst payout timing varies from 7 to 30+ days by firm

Prop firm FAQs

Which prop firm is best in 2026?

There is no single best — it depends on your style. Scalpers need tight spreads and fast execution; swing traders need generous drawdown. Use the free matcher to filter by your criteria instead of trusting paid reviews.

How do prop firm challenges work?

You pay an evaluation fee, hit a profit target without breaching drawdown limits, then trade a funded account and split profits (typically 80-90% to you). Rules differ per firm — always compare before buying.

Can you lose more than the evaluation fee?

With reputable firms, no — your risk is capped at the fee you paid. That is the entire appeal: trade large capital with defined, limited risk.
