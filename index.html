Methodology

A transparent standard for matching trader criteria with verified prop-firm information.

01

How matching works

Matcher compares the criteria a trader selects with the verified rules available for each firm. It does not recommend trades or guarantee that a firm is right for every trader.

Criteria firstMarket, account size, price, rules, and other selected constraints define the comparison.
Visible fitEach result will explain which criteria matched and which values were unavailable.
Trader decidesThe final choice always stays with the trader after reviewing the firm’s current terms.
02

How we rank firms

Rankings are based on disclosed comparison criteria, not paid placement or hidden star ratings. A score remains blank until the required source data is verified.

Rule alignmentProgram terms are compared with the trader’s selected priorities and constraints.
Data completenessMissing or unclear values reduce confidence instead of being estimated.
Clear rationaleEvery published position will include a concise reason for its place in the order.
03

How we verify

Firm information is published only after its rule, price, limit, source, and review date are checked. Unverified values stay unavailable rather than being filled with assumptions.

Source attachedEach published fact will be tied to the source used for verification.
Review date visibleUsers will see when the information was last checked.
Changes recheckedMaterial rule or pricing changes require verification before the listing is updated.
04

Our principles

PIPVANT is built as a decision-tool suite, with strict limits on what the product claims and displays.

Real data onlyNo invented statistics, prices, or performance records.
No fake listingsA firm appears only when its listing can be supported by verified source data.
Tools, not signalsPIPVANT supports research, sizing, journaling, and review. It never tells users what to trade.
No profit promisesCalculations and certificates never imply guaranteed returns.
