💸 The split is not the payout

An 80% split on $5,000 is $4,000 before anything else. Processing and transfer fees — whatever yours actually are — come out of that share, not out of the headline.

📊 Effective split tells the truth

After fees, an 80% split can behave like 76%. Comparing firms on effective split — at your typical payout size — is the honest comparison.

Net payout FAQs

How is a net prop firm payout calculated?

Your share is the gross profit multiplied by your profit split. Any payout fees you enter — a percentage fee and a fixed fee — are then subtracted. What remains is the net amount in hand, and the effective split is that net amount divided by the gross profit.

What is a minimum payout threshold?

Many firms only process a payout once your share reaches a minimum amount. If your share is below the threshold you enter, the profit stays in the account until the share grows past it.

Why aren't firm fees pre-filled?

Fees change, differ by payment method and are not published consistently. Rather than show you a guessed number, this calculator leaves fees as your own inputs — enter what your firm or provider actually charges you.
