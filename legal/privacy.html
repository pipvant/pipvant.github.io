Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

What we store

Without an account: your trading journal entries and risk calculator inputs stay in your browser's local storage — private to your device, never sent to us.

With a free account: we store your email address, your journal entries (including any chart screenshots you upload), your risk defaults, your firm watchlist and your saved comparisons, and your Pro waitlist email if you join it. This data lives in a secure database (hosted in the EU) and is protected by row-level access rules — only you can read or change your own data. We never see your password; authentication is handled by our infrastructure provider.

What we don't collect

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone. If you click a "Visit site" link to a prop firm, that firm's own privacy policy applies from that point on.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual journal entries, screenshots, watchlist items and saved comparisons at any time from the tools themselves. To delete your account and all associated data, write to hello@orovanta.com from your account email and we will remove everything within 30 days.

Questions about privacy: hello@orovanta.com.
