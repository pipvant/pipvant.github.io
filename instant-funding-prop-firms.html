What "instant funding" actually means — the fine print

  • No evaluation, no profit target. Pay the one-time fee and you trade a funded account from day one. None of the 62 tracked instant plans lists a profit target in its verified rules.
  • But every plan uses trailing drawdown. 57 tracked plans use standard trailing (3% at Maven Trading and Funded Trader Markets Instant Pro, up to 10% at FTMO); 5 more (Funded Trader Markets Instant Plus) use a trailing-lock variant. It follows your equity peaks — one bad day can still end the account.
  • Fees are one-time, but higher. Instant entry is priced above evaluation plans at the same account size — you pay for skipping the challenge. Promotions (up to 50% in the current verified data) can narrow the gap, but they carry expiry dates.
  • Payouts vary. Funded Trader Markets publishes on-demand payouts on its instant programs. The other tracked firms don't publish a payout schedule in their verified rules — PIPVANT shows "not stated" instead of guessing.

Instant funding FAQs

What is an instant funding prop firm?

You pay a one-time fee and trade a funded account immediately — no evaluation phases to pass. In PIPVANT's verified data, 8 firms offer 62 instant plans. Every tracked plan carries no profit target, but all of them use trailing drawdown, so breaching the limit still ends the account.

Do instant funding accounts have a profit target?

None of the 62 tracked instant plans lists a profit target in its verified rules — that is what makes them "instant". Payouts still follow each firm's published schedule; where a firm doesn't publish one, the table shows "not stated" rather than guessing.

What drawdown do instant funding prop firms use?

Trailing drawdown on every tracked plan: 57 standard trailing and 5 with a trailing-lock variant (Funded Trader Markets Instant Plus). The range runs from 3% (Maven Trading, Funded Trader Markets Instant Pro) to 10% (FTMO). Trailing follows your equity peaks, which makes it much harder to respect than a static limit.

How much does instant funding cost?

At a $50K like-for-like snapshot: from $170 (Maven Trading) to $2,198.90 (Funded7), with FTMO priced in EUR at €1,499 — all one-time fees, before promotions. Instant fees run higher than evaluation fees at the same account size. Always confirm current terms on the firm's official site.
