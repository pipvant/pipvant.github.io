Questions

Why go Pro?

If you journal every day, the Free limits will eventually feel small. Pro removes them and adds the analytics layer — mistake patterns, setup stats, session breakdowns, R-distribution — built for traders reviewing hundreds of trades. If you trade a few times a month, Free is genuinely plenty.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro will be month-to-month, or yearly if you prefer the discount. Cancel in one click from your account page — you keep Pro until the end of the billing period. No calls, no dark patterns.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Your journal, screenshots and settings stay exactly as they are. Free limits simply apply going forward — for example, you cannot add trades beyond the 100-trade Free allowance until you are under it again — but everything already saved remains readable and exportable.
