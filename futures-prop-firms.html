How to pick a futures prop firm

At the $50K tier the targets look identical ($3,000 profit, $2,000 drawdown) — so the decision moves to the fine print: drawdown type (trailing is much harder than static, and EOD trailing only moves at the close while intraday trailing follows every peak), consistency rules (a cap on how much of your total profit can come from a single day), payout speed (daily vs every-5-days changes your cash flow), and fee model (a cheap monthly fee compounds if you need three months to pass). Add the contract limits too — some plans cap how many micros you can hold, which matters for scalpers.

Most tracked futures firms do — EOD trailing is the common variant, and Apex also runs an intraday trailing plan. Trailing drawdown follows your equity peaks — significantly harder to respect than a static limit. Check the drawdown type badge in the table before buying.
