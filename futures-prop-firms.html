How to pick a futures prop firm

At the $50K tier the targets look identical ($3,000 profit, $2,000 drawdown) — so the decision moves to the fine print: drawdown type (trailing is much harder than static), payout speed (daily vs every-5-days changes your cash flow), and fee model (a cheap monthly fee compounds if you need three months to pass). Add the contract limits too — some plans cap how many micros you can hold, which matters for scalpers.
