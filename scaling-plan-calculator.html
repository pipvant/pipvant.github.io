Scaling plan FAQs

What is a prop firm scaling plan?

A scaling plan grows your funded account size in steps — for example +25% every 4 months — when you trade consistently. This calculator projects how those steps, combined with your assumed monthly return, carry your starting size to a target size.

Is this projection a promise?

No. Every number here is an assumption-based projection from the inputs you enter — your monthly return and scaling steps are assumptions, not guarantees. Real results depend on your trading and on your firm's actual scaling conditions.

Why does the timeline stop at 10 years?

Projections beyond 10 years compound assumptions into fiction. If your target is not reached within 120 months at these inputs, the calculator says so honestly instead of extrapolating forever.
