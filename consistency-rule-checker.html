🎯 Why firms cap your best day

The rule separates process from luck. A trader who makes $5,000 evenly across ten days looks fundable; a trader with one $5,000 lottery day and nine losses does not.

Consistency rule FAQs

What is a consistency rule?

A consistency rule limits what share of your total profit can come from a single trading day. If your firm caps it at 30% and your biggest day is $1,250 of a $2,430 total (51%), you cannot withdraw until the ratio fits.

How is the percentage calculated?

Largest single day's profit divided by total (net) profit for the period, times 100. This checker uses the net method — the most common one. Some firms count only winning days.

What if my biggest day is too large?

Keep trading normally — every additional profitable day raises the total and dilutes the largest day's share. The checker above tells you exactly how much more profit you need.
