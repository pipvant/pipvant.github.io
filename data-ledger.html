Why publish a change log?

Prop firms change prices, drawdown rules and promotions quietly — and a comparison built on stale data quietly misleads. PIPVANT re-checks its firm data against official firm websites on a regular schedule, and this ledger is the receipt: instead of asking you to trust a "verified" badge, it shows exactly what changed, when it changed, and the official page it was verified on. If nothing changed at a check, nothing is written here.

Ledger FAQs

What is the PIPVANT data ledger?

It is the public change log behind PIPVANT's verified prop firm data. Every row records one verified change — a price, a rule or a promotion — with the date it was found, the old and new values, and a link to the official firm page it was verified on.

How does a change get into the ledger?

PIPVANT re-checks firm data against official firm websites on a regular schedule. When a check finds a value that differs from the verified database, the change is recorded here with its source. Unverified or ambiguous findings are never published as changes.

Why is a firm or a date missing from the ledger?

The ledger only lists changes. If a firm's prices and rules matched the verified data at the last check, there is nothing to record — no news is good news, and it is not dressed up as an entry.
