Why payout terms matter more than you think

Two traders pass the same $100K challenge. One waits 14 days for the first payout, the other withdraws daily. After three months, the second trader has compounded both capital and confidence. When comparing firms, weigh three things alongside the evaluation fee: minimum trading days before the first payout, payout frequency after that, and how the split scales — a 50% starting split that grows to 100% beats a flat 80% for traders who stick around.

Payout FAQs

Which prop firm has the fastest payouts?

Per verified October 2026 data: TakeProfitTrader, E8 Markets (E8 Pro) and MyFundedFutures (Rapid) offer daily payouts. Topstep and FTMO pay on-demand after qualifying periods. Confirm current terms on official sites.

What is a good profit split?

80% to the trader is the standard baseline; many firms scale to 90–100% via add-ons or account growth. Apex pays 100% on Sim Funded accounts; The5ers scales from 50% to 100%.

How long until the first payout?

Widely variable — some firms pay within days of going live, others require 14–30 trading days first. FTMO: on-demand after 14 days. The5ers: bi-weekly after 14 days. Always check minimum trading days.
