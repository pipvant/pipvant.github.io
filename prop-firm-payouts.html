Why payout terms matter more than you think

Two traders pass the same $100K challenge. One waits 14 days for the first payout, the other withdraws daily. After three months, the second trader has compounded both capital and confidence. When comparing firms, weigh three things alongside the evaluation fee: minimum trading days before the first payout, payout frequency after that, and how the split scales — a starting split that grows beats a flat split for traders who stick around.

Payout FAQs

Which prop firm has the fastest payouts?

Per PIPVANT's verified plan data: TakeProfitTrader and Lucid Trading (select programs) offer daily payouts, and MyFundedFutures pays within 48 hours. Payout frequency is per-plan — confirm current terms on official sites.

What is a good profit split?

80% to the trader is the standard baseline; many firms scale higher via add-ons or account growth. Apex pays 100% on its tracked plans.

How long until the first payout?

Widely variable — some firms pay within days of going live, others require a minimum number of trading days first. Always check the minimum trading days for your plan.
