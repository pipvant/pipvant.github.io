How these deals are verified

PIPVANT checks firm pricing and promotions against each firm's official website on a regular schedule and stores only what the official page shows. A deal is listed here only while that promotion is present and unexpired in the verified data. Percentage discounts are applied to the plan's listed price exactly as shown — the original price stays visible, struck through, so the saving is never a mystery. When a firm ends a promotion, the deal is removed at the next verification pass. Always confirm the final price on the firm's official site before you pay.

Deals FAQs

How are the deals on this page verified?

Every promotion shown here is read from PIPVANT's verified database, which is checked against each firm's official website on a regular schedule. A deal only appears while its promotion is active and unexpired in that data — discounts are never invented or copied from affiliate roundups.

How is the discounted price calculated?

When a promotion states a percentage discount, the promo price is the plan's listed price reduced by that percentage. The original price is always shown struck through next to it so you can check the maths yourself on the firm's official site.

Do I need a promo code for every deal?

No. Some firms apply the discount automatically on their own site — those deals are labelled "No code needed". Where a code exists, it is shown on the card with a one-tap copy button.

Why did a deal disappear?

Deals are removed automatically once the promotion expires or is no longer found during verification. If a firm ends a sale early, the deal comes down at the next verification pass.
