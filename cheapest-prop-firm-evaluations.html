The cheapest sticker isn't always the cheapest path

Do the monthly math first: a $49/month subscription costs $196 if the evaluation takes four months — more than a $195 one-time fee. Then weigh the rules: a $22 evaluation with a 3% daily pause can be harder to pass than a $99 one with a 5% daily loss limit. And after you pass, payout terms decide your cash flow — daily payouts beat bi-weekly ones. Cheap gets you in the door; terms decide whether you stay. What is the cheapest prop firm evaluation?

The5ers' $2.5K entry at $22 one-time is the lowest verified fee, followed by Funding Pips ($36 for $5K) and Alpha Capital Group ($37 for $5K). Topstep's $49/month $50K plan is the cheapest monthly entry. Fees change with promos — confirm on the official site.

One-time or monthly — which is cheaper?

It depends on how long you take. Most firms charge once per attempt; Topstep and TakeProfitTrader charge monthly, so four months at $49/month costs $196 — more than many one-time fees. Multiply the monthly fee by your realistic timeline before comparing.
